Logging time

Use the bar at the top of every page. Switch between the two modes with the pills on its right.

  1. Timer: type what you are working on, pick a project and where you are, then press Start. Press Stop when you finish. The timer keeps running if you close the page.
  2. Add time: type the task, set From and To, or type the number of minutes (any number, like 5 or 7) and the end time fills in. Press Add.
  3. Log my usual day (if you have one) adds your official hours for the day you are viewing in one tap.

Add notes under the task for details. To change or delete an entry, tap ✎ next to it, or tap its block in Day, Week or Month. In Day and Week you can also drag a block to a new time (or another day), and drag its bottom edge to change its length. On a phone, press and hold first. ▶ restarts a task.

Where you worked

  • Office time at the office
  • WFH working from home
  • Field work out for work: bank, site visits, meetings
  • Break not counted as worked time; a break inside working hours is taken off your total
  • Leave and holidays annual, sick, unpaid leave and public holidays turn the day gray

You only see the options your admin has turned on for you.

Views

  • Tracker: your entries as a list, one day or one week at a time.
  • Day and Week: your time laid out hour by hour.
  • Month: the whole month with every task inside its day.
  • Reports: totals for the week or month, hours per day, and breakdowns by location, project, person and leave.

Weekend days are shaded lilac with a Weekend label. Use the arrows to move around and Today to jump back. Export CSV downloads what is on screen as a spreadsheet.

Official hours

If you have official working hours, TaDay compares them with what you log. You will see 6:30 of 8:00 on each day, a dashed Official outline in Day and Week, and a chip: +0:30 over, -1:30 under, On target. Weekends, leave and holidays count as 0.

To Do and Ta-da!

Add things to do, with a due date if you like. Tick the circle when it is done and it moves to Ta-da!, your list of finished work. Tap a finished item to move it back. ▶ starts the timer on a to-do.

Use it as a phone app

  • iPhone: open TaDay in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  • Android: open TaDay in Chrome, tap ⋮, then Install app.

Inside the phone app, sign in with your password (set one with Set password in the sidebar) or the 6-digit code from the sign-in email, rather than the link.

For admins

  • Team: invite people by name and email (Member, Viewer or Admin), resend links, remove or restore access.
  • Permissions: for each person, choose where they can log time, which pages they see, and their official hours.
  • Settings: working days, when the week starts, whether weekends show, and team defaults for new people.
  • Showing: at the bottom of the sidebar, switch between your time, everyone, or one person. Every view, report and to-do list follows it.

Problems?

  • No sign-in email: check spam, wait a few minutes, then ask for a new one.
  • Something looks out of date: refresh. The date under Sign out shows the version you have.
  • Can't see an option or page: your admin controls what you can see.
